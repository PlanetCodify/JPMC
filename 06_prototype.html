Why now

On April 17, 2026, the Federal Reserve issued SR 26-2, superseding SR 11-7, and explicitly excluded generative and agentic AI from formal model-risk scope for banks over $30B in assets. That leaves any AI-adjacent feature added to a digital channel — personalization, next-best-action messaging — without a standard federal governance path. This platform proposes an internal one, exposed as a service any market can call. See the Governance Simulator for how a feature gets classified.
Grounded entirely in public sources (Federal Reserve, JPMC press coverage, ICB public disclosures). Makes no claim to JPMorgan Chase's actual internal systems, vendors, or roadmap — see the accompanying research notes and strategy document for the full source trail.

Architecture

Three integration-pattern decisions, at black-box level. Toggle a path below to trace it through the system.

The three decisions

1. Sync only where staleness is dangerous. Consent state is written synchronously to one store before any send — the sole exception to an otherwise async, event-driven design.
2. Version the platform's own API, not the vendor's. A CDP or comms vendor swap happens behind the contract, invisible to market teams.
3. Governance is a callable service. Market teams request a risk tier the same way they'd call any other platform service — not a separate initiative.
